DATA PROVENANCE

What each status means

Official source

Dates reviewed against society or meeting pages during the October 2026 research pass. Always reconfirm before submitting.

RISE tracker

Imported from the provided RISE 2026–27 master tracker. These fields need an additional check against the current official page.

Source conflict

Official pages disagree or present ambiguous dates. We display the uncertainty and direct residents to the organizer.

Not announced

We do not invent deadlines or roll forward last year's dates as though confirmed.
