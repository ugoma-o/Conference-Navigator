DATA PROVENANCE

What each status means

Official source

Conference information supported by linked official meeting or society pages; individual fields may have different verification dates.

RISE tracker

Historical planning reference only. Where a current official source explicitly confirms a different date, the official source takes precedence.

Source conflict

Multiple official statements disagree or are ambiguous. Such discrepancies are not resolved by choosing a date from the original tracker.

Not announced

We do not invent deadlines or roll forward last year's dates as though confirmed.

AUTOMATED UPDATE PIPELINE

Weekly source monitoring

RISE checks tracked official conference websites weekly and follows a limited number of same-site abstract submission links. Explicit dates that pass validation may be published. Missing dates, inaccessible sources, and conflicting statements are retained in the weekly coverage report. Always confirm important deadlines with the organizer.
